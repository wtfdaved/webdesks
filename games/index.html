Ways to play

Online, in the room, or on the big screen

Post the join link in the Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet or Slack chat and share the game window. Perfect for remote team building and stand-up icebreakers.

On the TV

Cast the board to a Chromecast or Google TV, mirror it with AirPlay, or plug in a laptop. Everyone on the couch answers from their phone.

In a classroom

Put the board on the projector and let students join with the QR code — no logins to hand out.

FAQ

Good to know

Are WebDesks group games free?

Yes. The games are free to host and free to play. Hosts use a free WebDesks account so games are saved; players join with a link or QR code and don’t need an account.

Can we play over Microsoft Teams, Zoom or Google Meet?

Yes. Share the join link in the meeting chat, then share the game board window. Everyone plays along from their own device. Step-by-step Teams guide.

Can I put the game on my TV?

Yes. Cast the board from Chrome to a Chromecast or Google TV, mirror it with AirPlay, or connect a laptop with HDMI. Players in the room answer on their phones. Game night on TV guide.
