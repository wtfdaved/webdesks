      How it works

      How to use the online Magic 8 Ball

      1. Ask a yes-or-no question

        “Should I…?”, “Will…?” and “Is…?” work best. Ask “what” or “why” and the ball may tell you it isn’t a search engine.

      2. Shake, tap or press Enter

        On a phone you can shake the device. The ball wobbles and its answer floats up in the window.

      3. Share it (or ask again)

        Send the answer to a friend for a second opinion, or switch theme and see if the cat agrees with the dog. It won’t.

      The 20 classic Magic 8 Ball answers

      FAQ

      Magic 8 Ball questions

      How do I use the online Magic 8 Ball?

      Type a yes-or-no question in the box, then press Ask, tap the ball or shake your phone. The ball shakes and its answer floats up in the window.

      What are the Cat and Dog Magic 8 Balls?

      They’re themes. The Cat 8 Ball answers with cat puns like “Paws-itively yes” and “Not a feline chance.” The Dog 8 Ball is an excitable good boy: “Ruh-roh. No.” There are also Classic, Office and Random themes.

      Is the Magic 8 Ball real or random?

      It’s random, just like the plastic toy. Each answer is picked at random and leans yes, no or maybe. It’s for fun — please don’t use it for medical, money or legal decisions.

      Can I shake my phone to ask?

      Yes. Type your question and give your phone a shake. On iPhone, Safari asks once for permission to use motion (tap “Enable shake”); tapping the ball always works too.

      Is it free? Do I need an account?

      It’s completely free and needs no account or app. Your questions stay in your browser and are never sent to a server. It even works offline once you’ve visited.

      Can I use it in a meeting or classroom?

      Absolutely — share your screen and let the group shout questions. For a bigger group game, try Survey Says, our free survey game show for Teams, Zoom and TV.
